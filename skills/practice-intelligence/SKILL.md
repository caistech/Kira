# Practice Intelligence

## Purpose

Practice Intelligence is Kira's capability for identifying, researching and qualifying healthcare practices that may benefit from an AI receptionist, operational intelligence or broader agentic organisational intelligence.

The capability is designed to move from:

1. Practice discovery
2. Practice research
3. Technology-stack identification
4. Reception/workflow analysis
5. Decision-maker identification
6. Prospect qualification
7. Relevant outreach preparation

Kira provides the reasoning and intelligence.

The Orchestrator provides the execution layer.

## Core principle

Do not treat a medical practice simply as a lead.

Treat it as an organisation whose operating model can be understood from publicly available signals.

The objective is to identify:

- how the practice operates
- how patients interact with it
- what technology it uses
- where administrative friction exists
- who appears to own the relevant decision
- whether an AI receptionist or broader Practice Intelligence capability is relevant

## Separation of responsibility

Kira:

- interprets information
- reasons about the practice
- identifies patterns
- assesses fit
- determines likely decision-makers
- recommends an outreach angle
- produces the reasoning and draft

Orchestrator:

- executes searches
- retrieves websites
- retrieves job advertisements
- retrieves public practice information
- manages tasks
- manages approvals
- sends communications
- records outcomes

Kira must not assume that an action has occurred merely because it recommended one.

## Important exclusion

Healthengine practices should normally be treated differently.

If a practice is clearly using Healthengine, the immediate prospecting objective is generally not to approach the individual practice about an AI receptionist.

Instead, the opportunity should be recognised as evidence for the broader Healthengine relationship and potentially used to inform the strategic conversation with Healthengine.

The same principle may eventually apply to other platform partners.

## Strategic context

Practice Intelligence is not merely an AI receptionist sales tool.

It is an entry point into the broader Agentic Organisational Intelligence thesis.

The progression is:

AI receptionist
→ operational understanding
→ practice intelligence
→ organisational memory
→ agentic workflow execution
→ organisational intelligence

The immediate commercial use case may be reception automation.

The longer-term strategic value is understanding how the organisation actually operates.
