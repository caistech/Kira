# Decision Maker Identification

## Objective

Identify the person most likely to make or influence a decision about reception, practice operations, technology or automation.

## Preferred hierarchy

For independent practices:

1. Owner / Founder
2. Principal Doctor / Principal Dentist
3. Managing Director
4. Practice Director
5. Practice Manager
6. Operations Manager

For larger groups:

1. Group CEO
2. Managing Director
3. COO
4. Head of Operations
5. Head of Digital / Technology
6. Practice Operations Lead

## Evidence sources

Search:

- official website
- Our Team
- About
- Leadership
- LinkedIn
- company profiles
- public business information
- job advertisements

## Decision-maker scoring

### Owner / Principal

Very high relevance where the practice is independently owned.

### Practice Manager

High relevance for operational problems and implementation.

### Managing Director

Very high relevance where the organisation is a group or corporate practice.

### Reception Manager

Relevant to workflow discovery but may not have purchasing authority.

### Doctor

Relevant when clearly identified as owner/principal/director.

## Multiple decision-makers

If several people are identified:

- rank them
- explain why
- identify primary target
- identify secondary target

Do not send outreach to multiple people simultaneously unless there is a strategic reason.

## Contact confidence

HIGH:
The person is explicitly identified as owner/director/manager.

MEDIUM:
Role is strongly implied.

LOW:
Person is inferred from indirect evidence.

Never fabricate an email address.

If no direct contact details are available, recommend LinkedIn or the practice's public contact channel.

## Key principle

The goal is not to find "someone at the practice."

The goal is to find the person who understands the operational problem and can authorise a solution.
