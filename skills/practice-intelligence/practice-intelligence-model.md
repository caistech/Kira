# Practice Intelligence Model

## 1. Practice identity

Establish:

- practice name
- practice type
- location
- specialties
- ownership signals
- principal clinician
- practice manager
- relevant leadership
- website
- phone
- email
- booking URL

## 2. Operating model

Determine what can be inferred about:

- number and type of practitioners
- services
- consultation locations
- opening hours
- patient types
- appointment complexity
- administrative requirements
- specialist versus general practice model

## 3. Patient access model

Look for:

- online booking
- telephone booking
- enquiry forms
- patient portals
- SMS
- recalls
- appointment reminders
- telehealth
- after-hours arrangements

## 4. Technology model

Identify evidence of:

- Healthengine
- HotDoc
- Cliniko
- Best Practice
- MedicalDirector / Pracsoft
- Genie
- other practice management systems
- online booking platforms
- patient communication platforms
- translation systems
- website forms
- other automation

Do not infer a platform merely because it is common in that type of practice.

Record confidence and evidence.

## 5. Reception workload signals

Look for evidence in:

- receptionist advertisements
- medical secretary advertisements
- practice websites
- appointment instructions
- phone instructions
- cancellation policies
- billing instructions
- referral requirements
- theatre booking
- medical typing
- insurance administration
- multilingual requirements
- social media/content responsibilities

A receptionist job advertisement is particularly valuable because it exposes the actual work the organisation is paying a human to perform.

## 6. Organisational complexity

Complexity increases when a practice has:

- multiple doctors
- multiple locations
- multiple specialties
- hospital relationships
- theatre bookings
- allied health
- complex billing
- referrals
- multilingual patients
- significant phone traffic
- multiple administrative workflows

Complexity is not necessarily a negative.

It can indicate greater potential value from organisational intelligence.

## 7. Opportunity hypothesis

Kira should formulate a concise hypothesis:

"This practice appears to have [specific operational characteristic], which suggests [specific administrative problem/opportunity]."

The hypothesis must be based on evidence rather than generic AI-receptionist assumptions.
