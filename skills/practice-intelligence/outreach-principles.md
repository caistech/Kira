# Outreach Principles

## Objective

Turn practice intelligence into relevant human outreach.

The outreach must demonstrate that the sender understands the practice rather than sending a generic AI sales message.

## Structure

A strong initial email should contain:

1. Relevant observation
2. Why the observation matters
3. Specific hypothesis
4. Low-friction invitation

## Example structure

"I came across your receptionist position and noticed the role includes [specific responsibilities].

That caught my attention because it suggests your front desk is handling more than simple appointment booking — particularly [specific operational burden].

We're working on an AI receptionist / practice intelligence approach that is designed to take some of that workload away without replacing the human layer where judgement is required.

I thought it might be worth comparing notes for 15 minutes."

## Do not

- claim the practice has a problem that has not been evidenced
- use generic "AI is transforming healthcare" language
- imply the receptionist should be replaced
- exaggerate capabilities
- pretend to have spoken to the practice
- claim integration that has not been verified
- invent personal details

## Use the job advertisement intelligently

The job advertisement is often the strongest reason for contacting the practice.

Reference the actual responsibilities.

For example:

- phone management
- appointment booking
- billing
- recalls
- correspondence
- theatre bookings
- patient enquiries
- multilingual communication

## Multilingual opportunity

If the advertisement specifically requests a language such as Mandarin, treat that as an operational signal.

Do not frame the language requirement as a weakness.

Instead identify whether technology such as conversational translation could augment the existing team.

## Translation capability

Where relevant, Kira may recognise the relevance of the existing Tourlingo translation capability.

Do not claim a medical translation capability unless it has been explicitly verified.

## Platform-aware outreach

If Healthengine is identified:

Do not approach the practice with a generic AI receptionist pitch.

Instead capture the intelligence and consider whether it strengthens the strategic Healthengine conversation.

If HotDoc is identified:

Assess whether there is still an operational gap that could be addressed.

## Tone

Professional.
Specific.
Curious.
Low pressure.

The objective of the first message is to open a conversation, not close a sale.

## Approval

Kira can draft the outreach.

The Orchestrator controls delivery and any required human approval.
