# Prospect Qualification

## Objective

Determine whether a practice is worth pursuing directly, pursuing through a platform relationship, monitoring, or rejecting.

## Qualification dimensions

### A. Reception workload

Evidence of:

- high call volume
- appointment management
- cancellations
- confirmations
- billing
- patient enquiries
- administrative complexity

### B. Digital maturity

Assess:

- online booking
- PMS
- patient communication
- automation
- digital forms
- website capability

Low digital maturity can indicate opportunity, but does not automatically mean readiness to buy.

### C. Organisational complexity

Assess:

- practitioner count
- locations
- specialties
- procedures
- referrals
- billing
- theatre coordination
- allied health

### D. Technology compatibility

Determine whether existing systems create:

- integration opportunity
- platform dependency
- switching friction
- potential partnership opportunity

### E. Decision-maker accessibility

Assess whether a likely:

- owner
- principal
- managing director
- practice manager
- operations manager

can be identified.

### F. Strategic value

A prospect may be valuable because it is:

- a strong direct customer
- representative of a broader market
- strategically connected
- part of a larger group
- evidence for a platform partnership
- useful for product learning

## Qualification outcomes

Use one of:

DIRECT_PROSPECT
PLATFORM_PROSPECT
PARTNERSHIP_SIGNAL
RESEARCH_ONLY
LOW_PRIORITY
DISQUALIFIED

## Healthengine rule

If Healthengine is clearly present:

Qualification should normally become:

PLATFORM_PROSPECT

unless there is a compelling reason to pursue the individual practice independently.

## Strong prospect indicators

A particularly strong direct prospect may show:

- active receptionist recruitment
- multiple reception responsibilities
- significant telephone work
- complex bookings
- no obvious AI receptionist
- no Healthengine relationship
- identifiable decision-maker
- multiple practitioners or locations
- evidence of administrative strain

## Negative indicators

Do not pursue merely because:

- they employ a receptionist
- they have a phone number
- they have online booking
- they are a medical practice

The opportunity must be connected to an observable operational problem.
