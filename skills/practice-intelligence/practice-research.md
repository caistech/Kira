# Practice Research

## Research workflow

The preferred prospect discovery workflow is:

1. Search Indeed or equivalent employment sources for relevant receptionist/admin positions.
2. Open the job advertisement.
3. Extract the actual responsibilities and requirements.
4. Identify the practice/company.
5. Locate the official website.
6. Inspect patient access and booking mechanisms.
7. Identify technology/platform relationships.
8. Inspect the team/leadership information.
9. Identify the most likely decision-maker.
10. Construct a practice intelligence record.
11. Qualify the prospect.
12. Recommend an outreach strategy.

## Why job advertisements matter

A receptionist advertisement is a direct signal of organisational workload.

Extract:

- role title
- hours
- pay
- responsibilities
- systems mentioned
- phone workload
- appointment workload
- billing requirements
- correspondence
- recalls
- social media
- translation requirements
- software requirements
- locations
- complexity indicators

Do not simply summarise the advertisement.

Convert it into operational signals.

## Website research

Inspect:

- Home
- About
- Our Team
- Doctors
- Services
- Contact
- Book Online
- Patient Information

Look for evidence of actual workflows.

## Booking platform rule

Determine whether the practice uses:

- Healthengine
- HotDoc
- another external platform
- its own booking system
- no visible online booking

If Healthengine is clearly identified:

- do not treat the practice as the primary AI-receptionist target
- flag the Healthengine relationship
- preserve useful intelligence about the practice
- route strategic relevance toward the Healthengine relationship

If HotDoc is identified:

- record it
- do not assume that the practice cannot be approached
- assess the actual remaining operational gap

If no platform is identified:

- this may be a stronger direct prospect

## Evidence discipline

Every material conclusion should have an evidence source.

Use confidence levels:

HIGH
Directly stated by the source.

MEDIUM
Strongly implied by multiple signals.

LOW
Reasonable hypothesis requiring validation.

Never convert a hypothesis into a fact.

## Research output

Research should produce:

- practice profile
- technology profile
- operational signals
- complexity indicators
- likely pain points
- decision-maker candidates
- qualification score
- outreach hypothesis
