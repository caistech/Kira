# DIRECTIVE — Enhance Consultant Proposition on `/consultant-preview`

## Objective

Enhance the existing `/consultant-preview` page so that it clearly communicates **why a business consultant should want Kira / the BBBO ecosystem in their client relationships**.

This is a **messaging, positioning and presentation enhancement**.

Do NOT redesign the product architecture.

Do NOT create a new consultant workflow.

Do NOT change authentication, routing, database structures, agent behaviour, onboarding, dashboard behaviour or existing BBBO functionality.

The existing journey must remain intact.

---

# 1. Core positioning

The page should communicate that Kira is **not trying to replace the consultant**.

The consultant remains the trusted human adviser.

Kira provides a persistent capability that helps the consultant and the business owner:

- capture what is actually happening inside the business
- identify areas of owner dependence
- surface operational knowledge and gaps
- maintain organisational memory
- turn conversations into structured knowledge
- provide continuity between adviser engagements
- help demonstrate progress over time

The broader BBBO ecosystem then provides specialist capabilities for the different value gaps identified.

This is consistent with the BBBO model:

> The BBBO is the beneficiary.  
> The ecosystem provides the capability.

Consultants are one of those capability providers.

Do not position Kira as "the solution to everything."

---

# 2. Change the consultant value proposition

The page needs to answer the consultant's fundamental question:

## "What's in this for me?"

The answer should be substantially stronger than simply:

- "Kira helps your clients"
- "Kira captures knowledge"
- "Kira is an AI assistant"

Instead, frame the proposition around the consultant being able to **help clients build a better, more transferable business while strengthening the consultant's own relationship with that client.**

The consultant should see Kira as an additional capability in their toolkit.

The key story is:

### Better client outcomes

Kira helps uncover and maintain the operational knowledge and owner-dependence issues that are often difficult to capture during conventional consulting engagements.

### More valuable consulting conversations

Instead of spending valuable consulting time repeatedly reconstructing what is happening inside the business, Kira can continuously capture information between engagements.

### Greater client engagement

The owner has an ongoing mechanism for thinking through the business rather than only engaging when the consultant is present.

### Stronger evidence of progress

The BBBO framework ultimately seeks to assess, improve, measure and prove improvements in business value and transferability.

Consultants can therefore contribute measurable interventions rather than simply providing advice.

### A longer-term client relationship

The consultant becomes part of a longer-term business improvement journey rather than being associated with an isolated project.

### New opportunities

The BBBO ecosystem can create opportunities for consultants to address identified value gaps with their existing capabilities.

Do NOT promise guaranteed leads, revenue or client acquisition.

Use language such as:

- "can create"
- "can open"
- "potentially"
- "opportunity"

rather than making unsupported guarantees.

---

# 3. Introduce the "problems Kira helps solve" storyline

The consultant page should explicitly articulate the problems consultants encounter with mature owner-led businesses.

Use the following conceptual areas:

## Owner dependence

The owner remains the person everyone goes to.

Important knowledge exists primarily in the owner's head.

The business may perform well, but it is difficult to operate independently of the owner.

## Knowledge disappears between engagements

A consultant may identify important issues during a meeting, but much of the context, decisions and operational knowledge remain informal.

Kira provides a mechanism for continuously capturing that organisational knowledge.

## Consultants don't see everything

The consultant may only see the business periodically.

Kira can provide another layer of visibility into what the owner is dealing with between consulting engagements.

## Improvement needs to be demonstrated

For the BBBO mission, it is not enough to say that a business improved.

The objective is to:

**Assess → Improve → Measure → Prove → Sell**

The consultant contributes to the "Improve" component and potentially the evidence supporting it.

Do not claim Kira independently proves valuation.

---

# 4. Introduce the "benefits to you" storyline

Create a prominent section that effectively answers:

## "Why would I use this with my clients?"

Suggested benefit categories:

### Extend your reach

Kira can continue capturing information and prompting thinking between your consulting engagements.

### Deepen your understanding

Arrive at consulting conversations with a richer understanding of the issues the owner is facing.

### Increase the value of your intervention

Use persistent business knowledge to focus your expertise on the issues that matter most.

### Demonstrate progress

Create a stronger longitudinal record of what has changed, rather than relying entirely on anecdotal recollection.

### Strengthen client relationships

Become part of a longer-term transformation journey rather than a one-off project.

### Address bigger problems

The BBBO ecosystem is designed around the wider value gap — including operational, financial, management, technology, succession and other issues.

You do not need to solve all of those problems yourself.

---

# 5. Make the ecosystem concept clear

The consultant page should explain that Kira is part of something larger.

Do not make this feel like a generic "partner ecosystem" corporate page.

Make it practical.

For example:

**You bring the expertise.  
Kira provides the persistent business intelligence.  
The BBBO ecosystem provides the broader capabilities.**

The consultant can remain focused on what they do best.

This reflects the BBBO strategy: different ecosystem partners contribute according to their existing capabilities. Consultants specifically contribute operational improvement, strategy and performance capability.

---

# 6. Explain the BBBO opportunity without overwhelming the page

The consultant needs enough context to understand why this matters.

Introduce the larger proposition succinctly:

There is a generational ownership transition underway.

Many Baby Boomer business owners have spent decades building valuable businesses without deliberately preparing those businesses for eventual transfer.

The BBBO mission is to help 10,000 such businesses maximise the proven value of what they have built.

The first milestone is:

**1,000 businesses by 31 December 2026.**

The scale objective is:

**10,000 businesses by 31 December 2027.**

The consultant therefore has an opportunity to participate in a much larger business-improvement and ownership-transition ecosystem.

Do not turn the page into the full BBBO strategy document.

The page should create curiosity and understanding, not explain all 20 points of the strategy.

---

# 7. Position "True-Value" correctly

Use the term **True-Value** consistently with the BBBO proposition.

The objective is not to promise an inflated valuation.

The objective is to help create a business whose improved value can be demonstrated with evidence.

The BBBO framework is:

**Assess → Improve → Measure → Prove → Sell**

The consultant participates primarily in the improvement and evidence journey.

The ultimate proposition to the owner is **Maximum Proven True-Value**.

This language is supported by the BBBO strategy.

---

# 8. Position Kira's role accurately

Do NOT describe Kira as:

- an exit adviser
- a business broker
- a valuation engine
- a replacement consultant
- a complete business-improvement solution
- the BBBO ecosystem itself

Instead:

Kira is a technology capability that can help reduce owner dependence by capturing organisational knowledge, coordinating operational activity and creating persistent organisational memory.

That is the strategic role defined for Kira in the BBBO proposition.

# 10. Strengthen the CTA

The page should finish with a consultant-oriented CTA.

The CTA should NOT immediately ask the consultant to buy software.

The desired action is closer to:

**Explore becoming a BBBO ecosystem consultant**

or

**See how Kira could work with your clients**

or

**Become part of the BBBO ecosystem**

Choose the strongest option based on the existing page design and current journey.

Do not invent a new registration flow.

If the existing CTA already leads somewhere useful, retain the existing route and improve the copy around it.

---

# 11. Visual hierarchy

Rework the page hierarchy so the consultant immediately understands:

### 1. What problem are we solving?

Owner-dependent businesses are difficult to improve, manage and eventually transfer.

### 2. What does Kira do?

Kira captures and maintains organisational knowledge and helps reduce owner dependence.

### 3. Why does that matter to me?

It gives the consultant greater visibility, continuity and leverage with clients.

### 4. What's the bigger opportunity?

The consultant can become part of the BBBO ecosystem helping businesses improve and ultimately prove their value.

### 5. What do I do next?

A clear consultant-focused CTA.

The page should be understandable by someone who knows nothing about Kira within approximately 30 seconds.

Owner dependence, organisational knowledge and continuity of business intelligence.

### "Does Kira replace me?"

No.

### "What is the BBBO opportunity?"

A structured ecosystem focused on helping Baby Boomer-owned businesses maximise proven value and prepare for eventual ownership transition.

### "Where do I fit?"

As a capability provider who helps address specific business value gaps.

### "What's in it for my clients?"

Better visibility, stronger organisational capability, reduced owner dependence and a pathway toward greater transferability and demonstrable value.

Deeper client engagement, better continuity, greater visibility into the business and potential opportunities to participate in a much larger ecosystem.

There is one obvious consultant-focused next step.

Do not overbuild this.

The objective is not to create a new consultant product.

The objective is to make the existing proposition **obvious, credible and commercially attractive to consultants**.

The page should leave the consultant thinking:

> "This could make me better at what I already do — and give me a bigger opportunity with the businesses I already serve."

That is the outcome we want.
