## The ICP — read this first, it reframes everything

Given verbatim by the operator 2026-07-28 (memory: `project-kira-icp`):

> A **baby boomer owner, 60–70**, 30–40 years into a genuinely successful, profitable business, now
> thinking seriously about retiring or selling — **often privately, before they have told staff,
> family, or a broker.** The business runs on them; the know-how is in their head, not on paper, so a
> buyer isn't buying a transferable asset, they're buying the owner. Kira is a **voice-first** AI
> exec who works alongside them day to day and **quietly extracts how the business actually runs — by
> observing and doing** — turning "it's all in my head" into documented, transferable systems.

**Consequences that are easy to miss:**
- **Voice is the mechanism, not a §6 checkbox.** No audible Kira on the public site = the thesis is
  undemonstrable.
- **The Business Genome IS the promise.** "Out of their head onto the page" is literally it. A
  missing surface is the deliverable being absent, not a feature gap.
- **The doing-slice is the extraction engine** — Kira learns the business by doing the work.
- **60–70 sets the usability bar.** No icon-only controls, no jargon.
- **Privacy is existential.** He hasn't told his wife. A leaked valuation link is not a policy nit.
- ⚠️ **Anneke (advisor) is the CHANNEL, not the customer.** A run using only her tests the referral
  path and never the buyer. **Ray (66, exiting owner)** is now a mandatory persona in
  `~/.claude/skills/naive-tester/SKILL.md`.
