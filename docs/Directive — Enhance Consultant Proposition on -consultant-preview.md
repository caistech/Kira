# DIRECTIVE — Enhance Consultant Proposition on `/consultant-preview`

## Objective

Enhance the existing `/consultant-preview` page so that it clearly communicates:

1. **Why a business consultant should want Kira in their client relationships**
2. **Why the BBBO mission matters to them**
3. **Where the consultant fits within the broader BBBO ecosystem**
4. **How participation can strengthen the work they already do**

This is a **messaging, positioning and presentation enhancement**.

Do NOT redesign the product architecture.

Do NOT create a new consultant workflow.

Do NOT change authentication, routing, database structures, agent behaviour, onboarding, dashboard behaviour or existing BBBO functionality.

The existing journey must remain intact.

---

# 1. The central message — this is bigger than Kira

The page must not feel like a generic "AI tool for consultants" page.

The consultant should understand that **Kira is one capability inside a much larger BBBO mission**.

The strategic hierarchy is:

**BBBO Mission → Ecosystem → Capability Providers → Kira as one technology capability**

The BBBO is the beneficiary.

The ecosystem provides the capability.

Kira is one of the technology capabilities that can help address specific value gaps.

This distinction is important and must be reflected in the page.

The BBBO mission is focused on helping **10,000 Baby Boomer-owned businesses maximise the proven True-Value of what they have built and prepare those businesses for eventual ownership transition.**

The current mission establishes:

- **1,000 businesses by 31 December 2026**
- **10,000 businesses by 31 December 2027**

The first 1,000 are intended to prove the methodology, partner model, evidence model, marketplace proposition and commercial economics before scaling. 
Therefore the consultant proposition should be:

> **There is a large, specific and time-sensitive market of mature Baby Boomer-owned businesses that need help becoming more valuable, more transferable and more demonstrably ready for their next owner. Your expertise is one of the capabilities that can help make that happen.**

Kira makes that ecosystem more powerful; it is not the ecosystem itself.

---

# 2. Core positioning

The page should communicate that Kira is **not trying to replace the consultant**.

The consultant remains the trusted human adviser.

Kira provides a persistent capability that helps the consultant and the business owner:

- capture what is actually happening inside the business
- identify areas of owner dependence
- surface operational knowledge and gaps
- maintain organisational memory
- turn conversations into structured knowledge
- provide continuity between adviser engagements
- help demonstrate progress over time

The broader BBBO ecosystem then provides specialist capabilities for the different value gaps identified.

This is consistent with the BBBO model:

> **The BBBO is the beneficiary.**  
> **The ecosystem provides the capability.**

Consultants are one of those capability providers.

The mission explicitly identifies business consultants as contributors providing:

> **Operational improvement, strategy and performance.**

Do not position Kira as "the solution to everything."

---

# 3. Make the BBBO opportunity prominent

The consultant must understand **why this particular market matters**.

Do not bury BBBO in a paragraph near the bottom of the page.

Introduce it early enough that the consultant understands the strategic opportunity before reading the detailed Kira proposition.

The message should be approximately:

### A generation of valuable businesses is approaching ownership transition.

Many Baby Boomer business owners have spent decades building valuable companies without deliberately preparing those businesses for transfer.

The issues that constrain performance today can also constrain value and saleability later.

These include:

- excessive owner dependence
- undocumented operational knowledge
- weak management depth
- inefficient processes
- outdated technology
- poor data
- unclear responsibilities
- lack of documented systems
- succession risk
- limited visibility of true business performance

The BBBO mission exists to help address that value gap.

The mission is deliberately focused on this generation because an owner who may transition the business within the next **3–8 years** does not have unlimited time to address structural weaknesses.

The message is **not**:

> "Your clients are getting old and need to sell."

It is:

> **"They have spent decades building these businesses. Let's help make sure they get everything those businesses are capable of being worth."**

This is consistent with the core BBBO proposition.

---

# 4. Change the consultant value proposition

The page needs to answer the consultant's fundamental question:

## "What's in this for me?"

The answer should be substantially stronger than:

- "Kira helps your clients"
- "Kira captures knowledge"
- "Kira is an AI assistant"

Instead, frame the proposition around the consultant being able to **help clients build a better, more transferable business while strengthening the consultant's own relationship with that client.**

The consultant should see Kira as an **additional capability in their toolkit**, and the BBBO ecosystem as a **larger opportunity to apply that capability alongside their existing expertise.**

The key story is:

### Better client outcomes

Kira helps uncover and maintain the operational knowledge and owner-dependence issues that are often difficult to capture during conventional consulting engagements.

### More valuable consulting conversations

Instead of spending valuable consulting time repeatedly reconstructing what is happening inside the business, Kira can continuously capture information between engagements.

### Greater client engagement

The owner has an ongoing mechanism for thinking through the business rather than only engaging when the consultant is present.

### Stronger evidence of progress

The BBBO framework is:

**Assess → Improve → Measure → Prove → Sell**

The consultant contributes primarily to the **Improve** component and potentially to the evidence supporting it.

Do not claim Kira independently proves valuation.

### A longer-term client relationship

The consultant becomes part of a longer-term business improvement and transition journey rather than being associated with an isolated project.

### New opportunities

The BBBO ecosystem can create opportunities for consultants to address identified value gaps with their existing capabilities.

Do NOT promise guaranteed leads, revenue or client acquisition.

Use language such as:

- "can create"
- "can open"
- "potentially"
- "opportunity"

rather than unsupported guarantees.

---

# 5. Introduce the "problems Kira helps solve" storyline

The consultant page should explicitly articulate the problems consultants encounter with mature owner-led businesses.

## Owner dependence

The owner remains the person everyone goes to.

Important knowledge exists primarily in the owner's head.

The business may perform well, but it is difficult to operate independently of the owner.

Kira can help capture organisational knowledge and create more persistent organisational memory.

## Knowledge disappears between engagements

A consultant may identify important issues during a meeting, but much of the context, decisions and operational knowledge remain informal.

Kira provides a mechanism for continuously capturing that organisational knowledge.

## Consultants don't see everything

The consultant may only see the business periodically.

Kira can provide another layer of visibility into what the owner is dealing with between consulting engagements.

## Improvement needs to be demonstrated

For the BBBO mission, it is not enough to say that a business improved.

The objective is:

**Assess → Improve → Measure → Prove → Sell**

The consultant contributes to the improvement process and potentially the evidence supporting it.

The mission explicitly states that the objective is not an inflated valuation, but a business whose improved value can be demonstrated and supported by evidence.

Create a prominent section that effectively answers:

## "Why would I use this with my clients?"

Suggested benefit categories:

### Extend your reach

Kira can continue capturing information and prompting thinking between your consulting engagements.

### Deepen your understanding

Arrive at consulting conversations with a richer understanding of the issues the owner is facing.

### Increase the value of your intervention

Use persistent business knowledge to focus your expertise on the issues that matter most.

### Demonstrate progress

Create a stronger longitudinal record of what has changed, rather than relying entirely on anecdotal recollection.

### Strengthen client relationships

Become part of a longer-term transformation journey rather than a one-off project.

### Address bigger problems

The BBBO ecosystem is designed around the wider value gap — including operational, financial, management, technology, succession and other issues.

You do not need to solve all of those problems yourself.

That is precisely why the ecosystem exists.

Do not make this feel like a generic "partner ecosystem" corporate page.

> **You bring the expertise.**  
> **Kira provides the persistent business intelligence.**  
> **The BBBO ecosystem provides the broader capabilities.**

The mission explicitly states that ecosystem partners contribute according to their existing capabilities rather than every organisation being expected to do the same thing.

For consultants, their contribution is primarily:

**Operational improvement + Strategy + Performance**

> **You don't have to solve every value gap. You have a place in a system that can.**
